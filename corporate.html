Why now

AI can find the words. People still have to make them land.

AI can draft the deck, structure the pitch and prepare the talking points. It can’t walk into the room, read the person across the table or recover when the plan breaks.

That live part is a skill. Skills can be practised. BRAT trains them.

The familiar work moment

They know the work. Then pressure makes them rush, tighten up or lose the thread.

It happens in team updates, client calls and one-to-ones. Capable people hold back, over-explain or start again from the top. Nothing is wrong with their ideas. The moment gets ahead of them.

Int. Team meeting – 9:02 AM 1.

The update is ready. The slides are good. Every head turns to SAM.

Shoulders tighten before the first word. The opening comes out rushed. The main point gets second-guessed.

Manager

(cutting in)

Sorry, quick question before you go on…

One interruption, and the thread is gone.

What changes for employees

Six things people take into the next meeting.

Feel steadier before speaking

A short body-and-breath routine to settle before the meeting begins.

Speak up more comfortably

Voice, pace, posture and gesture that make the main point easier to follow.

Keep one thought clear

Focus on the person, not yourself

More people contribute.

Ideas land more clearly.

Less over-explaining, fewer restarts and shared cues for getting a conversation back on track.

How BRAT works

Prepare. Express. Notice. Adapt.

Every session runs the same practical cycle, adapted from how actors prepare for a live performance and applied to your team’s real work situations.

  1. Prepare

    Settle body and breath. Walk in with one clear purpose.

  2. Express

    Clarify the point and say it at a steady pace.

  3. Notice

    Listen to the response instead of rehearsing the answer.

  4. Adapt

    Adjust after an interruption and keep going.

Try the moment. Add one tool. Try again.

Practice you can observe. Every exercise gets a first and a second attempt, so people leave with a routine they can reuse at work, not a handout.

Informed by established acting, voice, movement and improvisation practices.

Safe to take part

Built for reserved and skeptical teams.

  • No characters.

    This is not an acting class. Nobody plays a role. BRAT adapts grounding, voice, intention and live response to real work situations.

  • No surprise spotlight.

    Activities begin in pairs or small groups. Nobody is put on the spot for a solo performance.

  • No forced sharing.

    No forced personal disclosure and no public employee assessment.

  • Agreed in advance.

    Participation and accessibility needs are agreed in the planning call, before anyone walks into the room.

Questions

What HR, L&D and team leaders ask first.

Is this an acting class?

No. Participants do not play characters. BRAT adapts actor preparation (grounding, voice, intention and live response) to real workplace situations.

Which program is the best starting point?

Choose SHOW UP READY for one preparation routine, READY IN REAL TIME for core employee skills, LEAD WITH PRESENCE for managers, RESET & CONNECT for an offsite, or REAL MOMENT LAB for one critical company situation. Not sure? Describe the situation in the form below and BRAT will recommend one.

What can be customized and what is included?

BRAT adapts the focus, language, examples and agreed work situations. Starting prices include one planning call, the selected program, participant materials and a short team recap. Deeper scenario design, additional facilitators, travel and follow-up sessions are quoted separately.

How many people can participate?

SHOW UP READY and READY IN REAL TIME support up to eight people. LEAD WITH PRESENCE supports up to four leaders. RESET & CONNECT supports up to 16. Larger teams need another facilitator or a second cohort.

What if employees are reserved or skeptical?

Activities begin in pairs or small groups. There is no surprise solo performance, forced personal disclosure or public employee assessment. Participation and accessibility needs can be agreed during the planning call.

What can one workshop realistically change?

One workshop can give participants a preparation routine, individual cues and observable first and second attempts. BRAT can track attendance, participant-rated usefulness and reported use after 14 days. Stable behavioural or business change requires repeated practice.

Where do sessions take place, and in which languages?

Onsite in Toronto and the GTA, or live virtual. Sessions are available in English, Ukrainian and Russian.
