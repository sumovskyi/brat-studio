The Familiar Work Moment

They know the work. Then pressure makes them rush, tighten up or lose the thread.

  • Their body tightens
  • They rush the opening
  • They second-guess the point
  • One interruption breaks the flow

What Changes for Employees

What employees can use in the next meeting or call.

Six practical responses to familiar moments at work.

01

Reset Before You Speak

Use a short body-and-breath routine before the meeting begins.

02

Slow Down the Opening

Start the first sentence at a pace you can sustain.

03

Use Body and Voice on Purpose

Use posture, gesture, volume and emphasis to support—not distract from—the message.

04

Make One Point Easy to Follow

Choose the main idea, finish it and avoid burying it in extra explanation.

05

Listen Instead of Rehearsing

Stay with the person and respond to what was actually said instead of preparing the next answer.

06

Get the Thread Back

Continue after a pause, interruption or unexpected question without starting over.

How BRAT Works

Prepare. Express. Notice. Adapt.

Every session follows the same practical cycle: prepare the person, clarify the point, notice the response, adjust and continue.

Practice you can observe

Try the moment. Add one tool. Try again. Leave with a routine that can be reused at work.

Simple, responsible measurement

Track participation, usefulness, readiness and intended use. Core programs can include a 14-day use check.

Informed by established acting, voice, movement and improvisation practices.
